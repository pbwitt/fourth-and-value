# Paid-ad reference — deferred

Prepared September 27, 2026. **Paid ads are on hold at the owner's request while
the site builds readership.** No ad budget, account, geographic audience,
placement, or platform approval has been established.

The material below is retained for a possible future discussion, not an active
plan. Do not pursue paid-channel research, applications, sponsorship quotes, or
campaigns unless the owner explicitly resumes this work. No traffic threshold
or future date automatically reactivates it. Recheck policies and pricing if
the work is resumed.

## What to promote

Promote the ability to inspect the reasoning: the forecast, sportsbook price,
research, and limitations. The NHL methods paper is a concrete first destination
with a PDF and numerical evidence. It suits an initial research-oriented
campaign without depending on a fresh morning card or a particular day's pick.

If the owner resumes paid ads, the first step would be to establish eligibility
for one channel, then consider a
small fixed-total test. Do not split a tiny budget among several networks. If a
platform's requirements or minimums make the test unsuitable, pause that option
rather than purchase a larger campaign.

## Illustrative seven-day test

This deferred example describes a possible modest test. No budget or geography
decision is pending while paid ads are on hold. **The $100 figure is not
authorization to spend.**

| Setting | Proposed value |
| --- | --- |
| Total | Up to $100 all-in, including any applicable fees; reduce media allocation if fees apply |
| Duration | Seven days beginning on the agreed launch date; no automatic renewal |
| Channel | One eligible, approved channel, selected after access and pricing are known |
| Audience | Adults 21+ in specifically approved U.S. locations, if the owner chooses the U.S.; exact locations still unset |
| Destination | Existing NHL methods paper; process page is a possible later test |
| Objective | Find readers interested in the research, not wagers, deposits, or a promised financial return |
| Creative | Two truthful messages below, adapted to the chosen format |
| Budget control | A verified campaign/lifetime cap or prepaid fixed placement; a daily average alone is not a hard total cap |
| Expansion | None during the first test; spending beyond the agreed total requires a new decision |

If targeting or a dependable spending cap cannot be configured as agreed, do not
launch. No paid scheduler, image generation, new subscription, or ad-management
fee is included implicitly. No research API budget pays for advertising.

## Measurement before spending

A repository search did not identify a conventional Google Analytics, Plausible,
Umami, or Cloudflare Web Analytics script in the relevant site code. This is not
proof that no external analytics service exists. No analytics account or working
visitor attribution was available to verify.

Use a distinct campaign URL for each creative once a destination and channel are
selected. Example only:

```text
https://fourthandvalue.com/research/nhl-forecasting-and-market-pricing.html?utm_source=x&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=nhl_research_pilot&utm_content=research_results
```

UTM parameters label traffic; they do not collect or report it on their own.
Before launch, verify that an existing permitted analytics setup actually
captures the campaign and intended actions, or explicitly restrict the test to
the platform's delivery/click metrics. No new pixels or cross-site tracking were
installed as part of this package.

| Measure | Definition / limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Actual cost | Platform or publisher charge, including fees |
| CTR and CPC | Link clicks / impressions, and spend / link clicks; platform definitions retained |
| Attributable visits | Campaign-tagged landing visits recorded on the site; not interchangeable with ad clicks |
| Engaged reader | If measurable: a visit with at least 60 seconds of active foreground reading, a PDF download, or a second research-page view; fix this definition before launch |
| Cost per engaged reader | Spend / measured engaged readers; undefined when the denominator is zero or unavailable |
| Returning reader | If measurable with the permitted setup: a later visit within seven days; report measurement coverage and attribution limits |

For a small test, compare the message with the reader response and record the
counts. Two ads and $100 do not establish a statistically reliable winner or
long-term acquisition economics. With no verified revenue-per-reader estimate,
there is no defensible promised advertising ROI or target acquisition cost yet.

Check delivery and the landing page once after launch and during the test. Pause
for an incorrect destination, broken measurement when measurement is the goal,
wrong targeting, policy rejection, or a spending-control failure. At the end,
report actual cost and available metrics without silently replacing missing
data with zeros. Continue only after deciding what those readers are worth to
the site and whether the observed response justifies another test.

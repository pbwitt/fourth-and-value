## Saved-data selection replay

Command: `python scripts/replay_top_picks.py`. Inputs are the checked-in source
boards/model context, with content hashes in `selection-replay.json`. Each sport
is evaluated one second after its own saved forecast time; these are separate
replays, not a claim that both feeds were simultaneously fresh.

research tier. A large eligible pool does not mean it can all receive paid review
within $2.75; pending coverage is explicitly exposed.

The formerly four-row MLB display now retains all 35 distinct qualifying bets,
including multiple markets in a game. These source rows still reflect the old
MLB upstream pick flags. The new EV-only hurdle takes effect at the next actual
MLB refresh, so this replay does not claim to count every newly qualifying offer.

These results establish selector behavior only. Models were not retrained here;
there is no new held-out predictive result, learned blend, profitability result,
or evidence that an LLM improves selections. New discovery is prospective shadow
research. The existing NHL empirical paper remains unchanged in its results.

## Remaining verification and operational limits

The new hosted-search route has not made a paid request on this branch. After
approval, supervise its first budgeted production run and inspect the archived
request/response, actual tool usage, validated source URLs and exact offer joins.
A conservative search reservation can exceed the remaining allowance; that is a
valid skip, not authorization to exceed the cap. Tests use mocked API transport,
not fabricated claims of live research success.

Source collection and model quality remain limited. Neither missing injury rows
nor sparse news proves normal participation. A quote can expire while research
runs. New published markets need a later feed refresh. NFL game research offers
have no established probability model; their quantity forecasts remain context.
Automatic tracker grading and structured human-intervention linkage remain
incomplete for the markets identified in `ANALYST_RESEARCH.md`.

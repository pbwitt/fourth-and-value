Separate the hockey forecast, market evidence and the price on offer.

Experimental forecasts, current prices

All seven markets remain available: shots on goal, goals, assists, points, totals, moneylines and puck lines. Models are selected with chronological predictive testing. None has established a betting edge against executable historical prices, so the recommendation list can remain empty. Market Watch is a research board.

Independent hockey models

Game forecasts use lagged, shrunk offense and defense rates and home advantage in a regularized goal-rate model. One joint score distribution produces totals, moneyline and puck-line probabilities. Regulation ties receive one overtime or shootout settlement goal. Empty-net behavior is included only in aggregate; starting goalies, injuries and travel distance are not verified model inputs.

Player forecasts separate projected ice time from per-minute production, with recency weighting and position priors for sparse histories. Negative-binomial distributions allow more variation than Poisson. Goals and assists share scoring intensity and add coherently to points. Player forecasts are conditional on participation and exclude shootout statistics. They do not establish an active lineup or power-play assignment.

What validation actually showed

Two validation seasons, 2023–24 and 2024–25, selected the models before the 2025–26 regular season was scored as the final test. The core team model improved joint-score log loss slightly but did not improve final-test moneyline log loss. Opportunity-based player forecasts improved count log loss in all four markets. Richer team context and boosting were less stable and were not selected. The full evaluation, historical predictions and limitations are in the repository’s NHL rebuild report.

Historical statistics were retrieved retrospectively. A next-day noon UTC availability assumption prevents same-game leakage, but original publication times and later revisions cannot be certified. These are reconstructed predictive tests, not historical execution or ROI claims. Playoffs are unvalidated.

Consensus and exact comparisons

We remove margin multiplicatively from two opposite prices at the same book, event, player, line and settlement profile, quoted within five minutes. Puck lines pair opposite handicaps. Consensus references must be within fifteen minutes of the offered quote. Each book gets one vote; the offered book is excluded from its comparison reference. Unmapped settlement rules prevent cross-book consensus.

Market probabilities are conditional on no push. Model win probabilities are unconditional, with push probability displayed separately. Expected value is win probability times net payout, minus loss probability. Integer-line market EV is unavailable without a push estimate. When one exists, the comparison explicitly uses the model’s push estimate.

Prices and uncertainty

Fair odds account for pushes. Minimum acceptable prices use a ±10% rate/opportunity scenario and a 2% EV buffer, including each scenario’s push probability. This is a sensitivity check, not a confidence interval or a proven threshold. A fixed monthly historical game-price sample did not establish superiority to consensus, and its three final-season shadow selections lost 1.52 units. The sample is too small to support a betting edge or learned blend. High payouts alone are not a reason to recommend an offer.

Analyst review

Goalie, injury, deployment and tactical evidence requires a source, publication time, review time and reason. Explicit manual adjustments are retained as unvalidated shadow overrides alongside the original forecast. The review records whether the information is already represented in features or market prices. No automated narrative creates a numerical edge.

Freshness and missing information

Only official regular-season games enter these boards. Quotes older than 24 hours, started games and failed market refreshes are hidden. Independent forecasts require recently checked game-level inputs and are withheld more than 48 hours before puck drop. Missing identities or models remain unavailable. Labeled historical references are separate fallbacks, never substitute model predictions.

Morning updates target 10:30 America/New_York, with an optional 16:30 refresh. Every published forecast and its inputs are archived for prospective grading; closing prices are evaluation information only. Related bets are treated as shared exposure and joint-bet probabilities are not calculated by multiplying marginal probabilities.

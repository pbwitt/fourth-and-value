Your morning process

  1. Check the offered line, price, minimum acceptable price and model uncertainty.
  2. Read the sources and the countercase. Verify participation, deployment and starting goalies where relevant.
  3. Check whether the news is already reflected in the model or market. Reprice if assumptions change.
  4. Record select, watch or pass with a reason. An exported review is a preparation note; it does not place a bet or publish a selection.

The fixed shortlist is saved before qualitative review. Human decisions and results are evaluated prospectively. The models and review process remain experimental; no daily pick quota applies.

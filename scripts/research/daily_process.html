How our daily picks are produced

We combine independent sports forecasts, available sportsbook prices and sourced research to build a list for human review. The process can return no bets. All three sports remain experimental for betting decisions; a completed review does not establish a profitable edge.

Policy: {{POLICY}} · All times are America/New_York, including daylight saving. This page is generated from the workflow configuration and checked for drift in pull requests.

The simple flow: Refresh sports data and prices → run the models → research the slate independently → combine the candidate pools → check the price and sourced context → publish up to 10 reviewed ideas → you decide and track.

When the work runs

These are scheduled starts, not guaranteed publication times. Jobs can queue, feeds can fail and books can post markets later. A research job waits for the relevant sports refresh when triggered by that refresh; it does not assume that a scheduled job has finished. The quote timestamp in the picks table is the sportsbook’s timestamp, never the page refresh time.

The order inside a successful run

  1. Refresh sports data and prices. The sports job retrieves its configured statistics, schedule and authorized odds feeds, retains stable identities and quote times, and archives inputs. This is not a claim that every injury, lineup or weather observation is a numerical model input.
  2. Run the sport’s model pipeline. Current inputs produce independent quantity forecasts and outcome probabilities where supported. NHL uses versioned trained models; MLB refreshes its cached daily training bundle as needed; NFL refreshes player/history estimates and its scoring projections. Missing models stay missing. The data and model update finishes before the review job consumes its published board.
  3. Research the slate independently. A separate AI-assisted search sees the available games and market types, without our model predictions or rankings. It looks for opportunity, matchup and participation questions. Suggestions must resolve to a real current offer with the exact game, player, market, side, line and book. Search suggestions are unverified leads until the underlying dated reporting is retrieved. Large slates are processed in bounded batches, with games submitted and source coverage recorded separately. A game being included in a search request does not mean every market or report was researched.
  4. Build the union of candidates. Keep offers passing the sport’s quantitative screen, plus independently researched offers. Neither route must agree with the other. Model quality gates, quote freshness and exact identities apply. The best available price is selected for an identical outcome and line. There is no four-pick quota and no one-bet-per-game rule.
  5. Collect evidence and investigate the case. Retrieve dated league/team reporting, injury information and relevant search leads. Check expected opportunity, starters, lineups, opponent effects, weather and settlement where evidence is available. Compare the projected quantity with the offered line, the model probability with the actual price, and the offered threshold with other books. Follow-up questions and unresolved source failures are retained. Professional betting recommendations are opinions, not verified support by themselves.
  6. Assess and rank. Each completed assessment says consider, wait for a specific material fact, or pass, with its case and countercase. Current consider assessments precede pending/wait/pass rows across all sports, so completed MLB or NHL opportunities remain visible above unreviewed NFL rows. Within an assessment status, each sport retains its own reliability and numerical ranking below; scores are not compared across sports. Related bets are labeled as shared exposure. No joint-bet probabilities are obtained by multiplying marginal chances.
  7. Build a manageable daily card. Show up to 10 current reviewed ideas, aiming for five to ten when the evidence supports them, with no minimum. Only current consider assessments no older than three hours or explicit analyst selections qualify. Explicit analyst selections take priority. Consolidate alternate thresholds and equivalent bets; keep their original forecasts intact. Wait, pass, unreviewed and changed-price assessments stay in the separate collapsed research pool. Additional reviewed opportunities also remain there. Multiple bets in one game are permitted but carry shared-exposure labels. This card limit is a workflow choice, not evidence of profitability.
  8. Publish the review record. Preserve the original model, offer, source times, review, exclusions and budget usage. The table shows the actual book’s line and odds, quote time, model and market comparison, review status and tracker action. An independent research candidate without an eligible model says “Not established”; it receives no invented probability or EV.
  9. Recheck and track. Later sports refreshes can add newly posted markets and changed quotes. Changed model/price inputs or newly collected evidence require reassessment. Unchanged price, forecast and evidence may reuse an assessment for up to three hours, preserving its original timestamp. A changed offer never silently inherits approval. Human bets can be logged in Bet Tracker; the site does not place wagers. Automatic grading is not connected for every supported tracker market.

The formulas and selection rules

Let d be decimal odds, p the unconditional chance of winning and u the chance of a refunded push. Then loss probability is 1 − p − u.

  • Expected return per dollar: EV = p × (d − 1) − (1 − p − u) = p × d + u − 1.
  • Fair decimal price: d_fair = (1 − u) / p. A price’s break-even probability, conditional on no push, is 1 / d.
  • Two-way de-vig: for paired opposite prices, q_i = (1 / d_i) / [(1 / d_1) + (1 / d_2)]. Only matching identities, thresholds, settlement and timestamp windows can be compared. A median line is not the book’s expected outcome.

Freshness, coverage and cost

NFL quotes expire after 90 minutes. MLB quotes and model checks expire after 90 minutes. NHL Top Picks quotes and forecasts expire after {{NHL_QUOTE}} minutes, with model inputs checked within {{NHL_MODEL}} hours. Started games, future-dated quotes and failed model feeds cannot masquerade as fresh model picks. The browser checks expiry every 30 seconds and reloads feeds every five minutes.

All Top Picks discovery and review share a ${{BUDGET}} per Eastern calendar day allowance. It includes search and review requests, legacy same-day charges and conservative reservations for failures with unknown usage. Morning spending is capped at ${{MORNING_BUDGET}}, preserving ${{LATER_RESERVE}} for noon-and-later checks. That is one daily allowance, not two budgets; unused morning capacity remains available later. Unspent amounts do not roll over. Existing odds-feed costs and the separate daily-article writer are outside this research allowance. No new paid subscriptions are enabled. A request is skipped if its conservative maximum cannot fit; this can leave money unused and candidates awaiting review.

Research batches rotate across sports. Independent discovery ideas receive an early review turn; later runs prioritize previously reviewed offers with changed facts or prices. This schedules research without assigning a higher win probability to those ideas. The main card holds at most 10 reviewed ideas. The separate collapsed research pool initially shows 20 additional offers, with a button to show all; all eligible candidates remain available for research. A research batch limit controls API payload size, not candidate eligibility. Budget and source limits can leave incomplete research. That coverage is recorded explicitly. No system can promise to examine every possible market exhaustively for $2.75, and no daily picks minimum is imposed.

One-day release test: The owner authorized a $10 shared research ceiling for September 27, 2026 only, including spending already recorded that day. It expires automatically at midnight Eastern; the usual $2.75 daily allowance resumes September 28. This does not change the maximum of ten reviewed ideas on the main card.

Ordering the daily card: After the completed-review gate, explicit analyst selections lead, followed by forecast reliability and an expected-log-growth score on a common scale across sports. The calculation uses the NHL ranker’s fixed 0.0025 fraction; it is an ordering convention, not staking advice. NFL uses the lower raw/calibrated estimate where available, MLB uses its win/push distribution and NHL retains its adverse-scenario score. Missing independent models receive no numerical score. Sport names and feed order do not receive priority, and no sport gets a guaranteed slot. This ordering has not been validated as a profitable selection strategy.

What remains to prove

New independent discovery and review decisions are prospective shadow records. We have not shown that this larger candidate pool, new ordering or qualitative intervention improves returns. Grade original forecasts and reviewed selections separately, retain correlated exposure and execution prices, and assess forecast calibration, returns, drawdown and closing-price diagnostics where trustworthy data exist.

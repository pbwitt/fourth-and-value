Reading these prices

Quantity changes the price

A small order may fit at the best price. Larger orders can reach more expensive offers. Our estimate uses the available price levels, rather than the last trade.

Fees are estimates

We use the observed series fee and event overrides. Each price-level cash cost is rounded up to cents, without rounding rebates. Your actual fees can differ. Missing fees stay unavailable.

Settlement must match

For example, some NFL ties pay 50¢ per contract rather than refunding your entry cost. A sportsbook comparison appears only after the exact outcome and settlement rules have been verified.

Equivalent odds describe the return if a contract pays $1. They are not a win probability and do not describe partial settlements. The board does not establish a model edge or add contracts to Today's Picks.

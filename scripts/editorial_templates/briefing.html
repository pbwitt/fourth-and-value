Before turning a number into a bet

Check the exact line and price together. Compare a quarterback's expected workload, a pitcher's availability or a player's role with what the original forecast assumed. A higher total at one book can be useful for an under, but the price may compensate for that difference. We do not rank these observations as positive expected-value bets.

Movement needs a timeline. A difference between two snapshots tells us what changed at the books we observed in both; it does not identify the reason. Injury news, roster changes, weather and trading can overlap. Follow the original reporting below before attributing a move to a single headline.

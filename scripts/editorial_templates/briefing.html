How this review list is selected

First, the numbers. Each sport compares its model forecast with the price offered by a book. We show up to four candidates per sport, one per game. The rankings are within each sport, and there is no daily minimum. Market Watch is separate: a difference between books alone does not qualify a bet here.

  • NFL: forecasts with fitted historical-outcome calibration and a model probability above the offered price’s break-even rate, ranked by that probability gap. The current screen has no upper discrepancy limit; unusually high estimates need scrutiny and are not established win rates.
  • MLB: markets that passed the existing predictive checks, at least 3% estimated return and a 3-percentage-point probability gap, at least two paired books, and the best observed price at the same line. Estimated returns above 30% are withheld for manual review.
  • NHL: an independent forecast, verified settlement mapping, at least 2% estimated return, and a price that still qualifies under the model’s adverse scenarios. Ranking accounts for that sensitivity.

Then, our analysis. We review dated reporting for relevant support, concerns and missing information. “Additional supporting context unverified” means the numerical screen flagged the offer, but the reporting reviewed has not established an additional case for it. It does not mean the model is absent, or that no relevant evidence exists elsewhere. Candidates remain on this research list while those checks are unresolved; a completed review does not automatically approve a bet or change its probability.

NFL’s model uses player history and calibration against historical results; current consensus is a separate comparison. Its calibration has not established a prospective betting edge. All three sports remain experimental for betting decisions. The model prediction is an expected quantity where available, not a guaranteed result or a market line.

NFL quotes expire after 48 hours under its current Top Picks policy; MLB model inputs and quotes after 90 minutes; NHL quotes and forecasts after 30 minutes, with model inputs checked within 36 hours. Started games, missing forecasts and failed feeds are excluded. The table rechecks expiry while open and reloads source boards every five minutes. The price time is the sportsbook quote timestamp, never the page refresh time. Changed offers require a recheck. MLB hitter props may appear later when batting orders are published.

Our context review uses AI-assisted research of linked reporting. Human verification remains required; model estimates and the original review are preserved.

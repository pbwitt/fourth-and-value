What do these checks show? Ratios of related sportsbook lines can differ from the model's assumed efficiency ranges. These are screening flags, not mathematically impossible outcomes or risk-free arbitrage. For example, Over 1.5 receptions plus Under 6.5 yards can both lose if a player makes one 10-yard catch.

The question is whether Philadelphia can sustain drives

Philadelphia enters Sunday’s London matchup after going 0-for-12 on third down against the Rams, according to the NFL’s October 9 preview. That is the most concrete offensive warning in the available reporting: an entire game without a third-down conversion. Jacksonville, meanwhile, arrives with consecutive wins over New England and Cincinnati. Those contrasting performances give the Eagles–Jaguars total a specific football question rather than a generic clash of recent form.

For Fourth & Value, the issue is whether Philadelphia can recover enough possession continuity to contribute to the combined score. Third-down conversions can extend scoring opportunities and keep an offense from repeatedly surrendering possession. But one failed game does not establish Philadelphia’s expected conversion rate on Sunday. Nor does Jacksonville’s winning streak, by itself, establish how many points this matchup should produce. CBS’s game-day preview places the teams at 2-2 and 3-1, respectively, with kickoff scheduled for 9:30 a.m. ET at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

The market has narrowed as well as fallen

Our Sunday morning snapshot has a median total of 41.5, with ten of eleven observed books at that number and BetRivers at 41. The median is down half a point from the October 10 at 6:32 PM EDT comparison. In that earlier snapshot, six books were at 42 and five at 41.5. This is stored movement between two observations, not an opening-line history.

The useful signal is convergence: the observed range has shifted from 41.5–42 to 41–41.5, with most books now sharing one threshold. That shows a lower and more concentrated market, not why it moved. The league preview discusses Philadelphia’s offensive difficulties and roster disruption, but we do not have a timestamped sequence connecting particular news to particular repricing. Attributing this decline to one development would exceed the evidence.

Threshold and price must be considered together

The current quotes illustrate why a single headline total is incomplete. Bovada lists over 41.5 at -105 and under 41.5 at -115; FanDuel lists the same threshold with over -115 and under -105. BetRivers offers 41, with over -113 and under -108. These observations were recorded between 7:09:14 AM EDT and 7:09:27 AM EDT on Sunday.

An exact 41-point result pushes at BetRivers but loses an over 41.5 elsewhere. Conversely, an under 41.5 wins on that result while under 41 pushes. The different prices also change the cost of expressing the same directional view. Without a validated outcome distribution, neither the half-point difference nor the cheaper quoted side establishes which contract is preferable.

The countercase keeps this on the watchlist

The serious countercase to a scoring rebound is that Philadelphia’s stalled possessions persist, leaving Jacksonville to supply too much of the total. The countercase to that pessimism is equally important: 0-for-12 is a description of one game, not a forecast, and improved drive continuity could restore opportunities without requiring an exceptional offensive performance.

The conclusion would change with verified game-day availability, a fresh matchup estimate with documented adjustments, or a materially different threshold and price. The NFL preview mentions lost starters but does not establish complete current injury status in the available account. For now, the lower total and dated projection justify monitoring Philadelphia’s scoring contribution—not a forced pick.

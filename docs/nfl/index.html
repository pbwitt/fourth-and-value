1. Check the timing

Confirm the game has not started and read the sportsbook quote timestamp. A new page build does not make an old price current.

2. Compare the same bet

Over 49.5 and Over 54.5 are different bets. Choose your sportsbooks first; “best price” compares an identical player, market, side and line.

3. Read the evidence

A probability fitted to past results does not prove future profitability. Missing player evidence is shown explicitly, and unsupported estimates stay out of Top Picks.

What does an edge mean?

At −110, a bet needs to win about 52.38% of settled decisions to break even. If a model estimates 55%, the probability edge is about 2.62 percentage points, or 262 basis points. Without pushes, the estimated profit is $5 per $100 risked.

That calculation is only as useful as the model probability. Check recent role changes, availability, price timing and the model's coverage before interpreting it.

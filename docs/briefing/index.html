How this review list is selected

The list uses each sport's existing model selection rules, then shows up to four candidates per sport and one per game. NFL candidates follow the Top Picks model-edge ranking; MLB follows its model expected-return ranking; NHL follows its sensitivity-based shortlist ranking. Rankings are within each sport. There is no daily minimum and market-only price differences do not qualify.

NFL quotes expire after 48 hours under its current Top Picks policy; MLB model inputs and quotes after 90 minutes; NHL quotes and forecasts after 30 minutes, with model inputs checked within 36 hours. Started games, missing forecasts and failed feeds are excluded. The table rechecks expiry while open and reloads source boards every five minutes. The price time is the sportsbook quote timestamp, never the page refresh time.

NHL context research is shown separately from your decision. A supported narrative is not analyst approval or a numerical adjustment. NBA will be added later.

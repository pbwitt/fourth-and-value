Placing a pair

  1. Pick a Locked pair whose after-tax result is positive at your settings.
  2. Open both sportsbooks. Confirm the same game, player, market and line, and that each price is the one shown or better.
  3. Bet the price that stands out from the other books first; it is the one most likely to move. Then bet the other side.
  4. Use the stakes shown for your total. If the second price has moved, decide before betting whether to take it, hedge elsewhere, or hold.
  5. Press Track on each bet to record it in Bet Tracker with the price you actually got.
